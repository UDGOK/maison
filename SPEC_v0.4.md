## A. Hardware — handheld POS (decision, documented in docs/hardware.md)
Primary handheld: **Verifone V660p on Stripe Terminal** (built-in thermal printer, ~10 h battery, Wi-Fi, 5.5" touch; driven by our JS SDK integration: `terminal.collectPaymentMethod` + `terminal.print(canvas)`). Associates carry an iPhone running the Maison PWA (v0.2 phone layout) paired to a V660p, or the V660p sits at a roaming station with an iPad. Alternative: Stripe Reader S710 (15 h battery, no printer) + Epson TM-P20II belt printer (ePOS over Wi-Fi). Clover Flex is NOT compatible (Fiserv-locked). Implement in frontend: a "Reader printer" print route — render the 80mm receipt to a 384-px-wide monochrome canvas and call `terminal.print(canvas)` when the connected reader `device_type === 'verifone_v660p'`; fall back to ePOS/window.print. Multiple readers per boutique: reader registry in Maison Boutique (child table `readers`: label, stripe_reader_id, device_type, has_printer) and a reader picker in Settings persisted per device. Simulated reader gains a `has_printer` flag so e2e can exercise the canvas print path.

## E. Itemized returns & exchanges
POS "Returns" flow: scan receipt QR / enter invoice no / search by client → list lines → select lines + qty + reason (Select: Change of mind, Defect, Sizing, Gift return, Other) + condition (Sellable / Damaged) → refund method (original card via Stripe refund by PaymentIntent, cash, store credit = ERPNext Credit Note left unallocated, or exchange). Exchange: return lines + new lines in one transaction; difference charged/refunded. Backend `sales.return_items(invoice, lines, reason, refund_method)` creates Sales Invoice `is_return=1` (credit note) with `update_stock`, serial back to stock when sellable (else to a "Damaged" warehouse per boutique), Stripe refund for card with `maison_terminal_ref`, commission reversal, loyalty points reversal; `sales.exchange(...)`. Manager PIN required over a configurable threshold (`returns_manager_threshold`). Return receipt print format. Policy windows (days) in POS Settings. Reports: "Returns by reason / boutique / associate".

## H. AI & insights (python, scheduled + on-demand; optional LLM narrative)
`maison_pos/insights/`:
1. **Affinity / next-best-offer**: item co-purchase matrix (lift) from Sales Invoices + client purchase history → `insights.recommend_for_client(customer, n)` and `insights.recommend_for_basket(items, n)`; POS shows "Suggested for this client" (3 tiles) in the client panel and "Pairs well with" under the basket; weekly cached table `Maison Client Recommendation`.
2. **Client patterns**: per client: cadence (avg days between visits), expected next visit date, spend trend, preferred department/metal, churn risk score (recency vs cadence), birthday/anniversary within 30 days → `Maison Client Signal` rows; dashboard "Clients to contact this week" list with reason; endpoint `insights.client_signals(boutique)`.
3. **Product performance by store**: per item × boutique: sell-through, days-on-hand, velocity (units/week), stock-out risk, vs chain average; "rebalance suggestions" (move N units from A to B) → `Maison Rebalance Suggestion` doctype with one-click "Create Material Transfer" (manager). Dashboard heatmap item-group × boutique and a "Top / slow movers by boutique" table. Endpoint `insights.product_performance(period)`.
4. **Narrative (optional)**: if `anthropic_api_key` in site_config, `insights.narrative(period)` produces a weekly plain-English summary from the computed numbers (never from raw PII) stored in `Maison Insight Report`; otherwise a template-based summary. Scheduled weekly Monday 06:00 site tz; emailed to Head Office role.
Seed: generate 6 months of plausible historical sales across the 3 boutiques (deterministic, ~1,500 invoices, respecting serials) so reports/insights have data. Make seeding of history a separate function `seed_history(months=6)` (also whitelisted `seed_history_remote`) because it's slow.
