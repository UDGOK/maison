# AWANZ POS — v0.5 "Salon & Command" (contract)

Read SPEC.md … SPEC_v0.4.md first. Additive; keep all suites green. Monolith Gold. Performance is a first-class requirement in this release: the head office will watch 40–100 boutiques at once.

## K. Client-facing screen — "AWANZ Salon" (second iPad / mini iPad facing the client)
A separate PWA route `/salon` (same app bundle, own layout) running on a client-facing iPad mini mounted at the counter, paired to a POS device. Pairing: Settings on the POS shows a 6-digit pairing code; the Salon enters it (or scans a QR shown on the POS) → `salon.pair(code)` → both devices share a `AWANZ Salon Session` (boutique, pos_device_id, salon_device_id). Transport: Frappe realtime room `awanz_salon:<pos_device_id>` (socket.io) for live mirroring + REST fallback polling (2 s) when sockets are unavailable; works on LAN only via the server (no peer-to-peer needed). The POS publishes `salon_state` events (idle / client / basket / pay / receipt / consent / feedback) with the minimal payload; the Salon renders.
Screens (design them like a luxury house's private salon, not a kiosk — black/gold, large serif-free Unbounded numerals, slow gold transitions, no clutter, 4:3 portrait or landscape both supported):
1. **Idle / Ambient**: boutique wordmark + a slow, generative "light on metal" animation (Canvas/WebGL, reduced-motion aware), the time, "Welcome to AWANZ Oak Street". Optionally rotates curated pieces (image, name, one line) from a `AWANZ Salon Playlist` (HQ-managed, per boutique or global) — the "novelty": pieces seem to float in and out.
2. **Welcome / Identify**: when the POS starts a sale with no client: "Are you a client of the house?" → enter phone/email/client № on a large gold keypad, scan your client QR, or "Join AWANZ" (sign-up: name, phone or email, birthday optional, consent checkboxes for email/SMS marketing, and the optional face-recognition consent flow from v0.3 shown here instead of on the POS). Creates/links the Customer and attaches it to the POS sale in real time.
3. **Basket mirror**: as the associate adds pieces, the Salon shows the piece large (image or generated visual), name, serial, certificate line, price, running total with tax; points to be earned; a discreet "Ask about this piece" button that drops a note into the CRM interaction timeline (questions from the client).
4. **Payment**: "Please present your card to the terminal" state with the amount; then "Approved" with a gold pulse.
5. **Receipt & feedback**: thank-you with the client's name, points earned and new balance/tier progress, QR to the public receipt, "Email my receipt" (enters/uses email), then the private feedback 1–5 (posts to AWANZ Feedback) and an optional "Would you like an invitation to our next private viewing?" (sets a CRM flag). Returns to Ambient after 20 s.
6. **Questions / Concierge mode** (selectable by associate): a guided Q&A to capture preferences into the Client Profile (ring size via an on-screen ring sizer visual, wrist size, metal preference, occasions coming up, style cards to tap) — turns dead time into clienteling data.
Backend: `AWANZ Salon Session` doctype, `AWANZ Salon Playlist` (+ child items), endpoints `salon.pair`, `salon.state` (poll), `salon.identify(phone/email/client_number)`, `salon.signup(...)` (reuses customers.upsert + consent), `salon.ask(question)`, `salon.feedback`, `salon.preferences(answers)`; POS publishes via `salon.publish(event, payload)` (server re-broadcasts to the room). Privacy: the Salon never shows the client's full phone/email (mask), never shows other clients' data, session expires after 12 h or unpair.
Frontend: `frontend/src/salon/` (router child app; `views/Salon*.vue`), Pinia `salon` store on the POS side that mirrors cart/client/pay state into publish calls (debounced 150 ms), Settings "Client display" card (pair/unpair, preview). Mock parity (a mock "virtual salon" pane in dev). Tests: state reducer, masking, pairing code TTL; e2e: two browser contexts (POS + Salon) walk a sale and assert each Salon state, sign-up from the Salon attaches the client on the POS, feedback reaches HQ.

## L. Head-office dashboard v2 — "Command" (dashboard/)
Rebuild the dashboard's information architecture for 40–100 boutiques with tabs: **Live**, **Boutiques**, **Products**, **Clients**, **Insights** (existing), **Reports** (existing links).
- **Live** (default): KPI strip (net, invoices, card/cash, avg ticket, returns, pending approvals, low-stock, open feedback ≤2); **Top boutiques today** ranked list (net, vs same weekday last week %, tickets, last sale time) — the **live feed is store-level**: each boutique row is a live card that pulses on a sale and shows "Sold · Perpetual 41 · $86,500 · 2 s ago" inline, plus a compact ticker of the latest 10 sales chain-wide; selecting a boutique expands its own item-level feed + hourly bars. A "Region" filter and search (boutique code/name). Render must stay smooth at 100 boutiques: virtualised lists (only visible rows render), realtime events batched with requestAnimationFrame, per-boutique aggregates updated incrementally from socket events instead of refetching; full refetch only every 60 s or on reconnect.
- **Boutiques**: table with sortable columns (net today / WTD / MTD, vs LW, tickets, avg ticket, conversion (sales / clients identified), returns %, stock value, low-stock count, associates on shift, status online/offline/queued), sparkline 14 days, drill-in page per boutique (hourly, top items, associates, recent sales, alerts, feedback).
- **Products**: two sub-tabs exactly as the client asked: **Trending in stores** (chain-wide items ranked by velocity change: units this 7 d vs previous 7 d and vs 28 d baseline, with "trending up / new / cooling" badges, group filter, store count where it's selling, sell-through, days-on-hand) and **Top products by store** (boutique selector or "all" → per-boutique top 10 by net and by units with share of boutique sales; a matrix view item-group × boutique using the existing heatmap). Both read from a server-side precomputed table refreshed every 15 min (`AWANZ Product Trend` rows: item, boutique|ALL, period, units, net, velocity, delta_pct, rank) so the page loads in < 300 ms even with 100 boutiques; endpoint `dashboard.product_trends(scope, boutique?, group?, period)` and `dashboard.top_products(boutique|all, by=net|units, period)`.
- **Clients**: churn-risk list for top tiers (from AWANZ Client Signal, filter Patron/Collector), follow-up rates per associate (tasks completed / assigned, 30 d), upcoming dates, recognition stats.
Backend: `api/dashboard.py` v2 endpoints with boutique-scoping; `insights/trends.py` computing `AWANZ Product Trend` (scheduler every 15 min; also on-demand `compute_trends`); `dashboard.live_summary` upgraded to return per-boutique `vs_last_week_pct`, `last_sale` summary (item, amount, ts) and to be cheap (single grouped SQL, cached 5 s in frappe.cache). Realtime payload for `awanz_sale` must include boutique, amount, top item name, customer tier (no PII). Tests for trend math and for live_summary correctness with returns; a load test script that seeds 100 boutiques × 20 sales in a temp site is optional — at minimum a python benchmark asserting `live_summary` < 150 ms on the 3-boutique site with 6 months of history and `product_trends` < 100 ms.
Dashboard frontend: keep Monolith Gold; typography scale for a wall screen (1920×1080 and 3840×2160) and for a laptop; `dataviz` discipline (gold fills, faint grids, emphasised endpoint). Screenshots at 1920×1080 with the seeded data; e2e: a sale in the POS updates the right boutique card and the ticker within 1 s.

## M. HQ intelligence completion — campaign attribution + associate/clienteling KPIs
- **Campaign attribution**: `AWANZ Campaign` doctype (name, channel Email/SMS/Event/Private viewing, audience segment definition (tier, boutique, item affinity, signal type), send date, content link, UTM/campaign code, coupon link optional) + `AWANZ Campaign Touch` (campaign, customer, channel, sent_at, opened_at, clicked_at, source: Frappe Email Campaign / Klaviyo / Brevo webhook). Attribution rule (configurable, default): a sale is attributed to the most recent touch for that customer within 14 days (last-touch), with "assisted" credit for other touches in 30 days; item-level attribution when the campaign featured the item. Nightly job writes `AWANZ Campaign Attribution` (invoice, campaign, type Direct/Assisted, amount). Report + dashboard Clients/Insights card "Campaign performance" (sends, opens, clicks, attributed revenue, ROI if cost entered). Inbound webhooks `campaigns.webhook_klaviyo` / `webhook_brevo` (signed) and an outbound `campaigns.export_segment(campaign)` → CSV/Frappe Email Group so the client can send from any tool and still close the loop. Seed: 3 campaigns with touches that line up with some history invoices so attribution is visible.
- **Associate performance & follow-up rates**: finalize `hr.employee_performance` to include clienteling follow-up rate (CRM tasks completed/assigned 30 d), clients identified per sale, avg ticket vs boutique avg, returns rate, recognition enrolments; expose in dashboard Clients/Boutiques tabs.
- **Churn for top tiers**: ensure `AWANZ Client Signal` "VIP lapsing" has an owner (preferred associate) and a one-tap "Assign call" that creates a CRM Task; dashboard list shows it.
